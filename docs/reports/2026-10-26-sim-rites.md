# Rite sweep simulation (2026-10-26)

Greedy bot (`chooseBotCommand`), two acts (`ACT_RULES.acts` = 2), seed 1, 200 runs per party composition (10 compositions, 2000 runs per Rite). Rite is applied with `applyRite` (GDD §13.3). Produced by `bun run sim -- --runs 200 --seed 1 --rite N` for N = 0..10. Raw outputs are in the session scratchpad, not the repo.

## Flags

Noise: with 2000 runs per Rite and a win rate near 15–28%, the 95% interval is about ±2 points. Steps smaller than that are not evidence of a trend.

| Step | Δ win | Flag | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | -11.2 | Too steep (> 8 points) | Real. Act 1 cleared also drops 11 points. Rite 2 (common enemies +15% damage) is the cause. |
| 2 → 3 | 0.0 | Not decreasing | Within noise. Rite 3 (start with 30 gold) is a relief rule, so a flat step is expected by design. Act 1 cleared still falls 2.4 points. |
| 9 → 10 | +0.1 | Non-monotonic | Within noise (±2). Not a real reversal. Rite 10 (boss phase 2 gets a fourth intent) changes only the boss fight, which this run count cannot resolve. |
| 8 → 9 | -0.1 | Not steep, nearly flat | Within noise. Rite 9 (injury −35%) only matters for injured heroes; the effect is small here. |

Not flagged: 0 → 1 (-1.9), 3 → 4 (-1.0), 4 → 5 (-1.1), 5 → 6 (-0.9), 6 → 7 (-1.7). Step 7 → 8 (-6.6) is a real drop but under the 8-point limit, so not flagged as steep.

## Proposed tweaks (not applied)

Double/halve rule: a number that looks wrong is halved or doubled first, then refined. None of these have been applied to `src/data/` or `src/core/`.

1. Rite 2, common enemies +15% damage (flag: steep, step 1 → 2). Halve the bonus to +7.5% and re-run 200 runs, then 1000 per composition before accepting. Expected: a drop of about 5–6 points, which would leave the step inside the 8-point limit. Where the rule lives: `applyRite` (`src/core/`, rite table in `src/data/`).
2. Rite 3, start with 30 gold (flag: flat, step 2 → 3). This is a boon, so the ladder is not strictly monotone by design. Decide whether the ladder must be strictly decreasing. If yes, halve the gold to 15 and re-run. If no, record the flat step as accepted.
3. Rite 10 (flag: +0.1, noise). No data change. Re-run Rites 9 and 10 with 1000 runs per composition before judging the order.
4. Rite 9 (flag: nearly flat). No change proposed. The rule matters only to injured heroes, so the sim may underweight it.

## Caveats

- Greedy bot, not a human. It has no card-level judgement and does not use Auto's full policy; absolute win rates are not a prediction of player win rates.
- One seed (1). Rerunning with another seed changes each Rite's value by about 2 points.
- Runs per composition are 200 (run time about 10 s per Rite). No run was shortened.
- Act 2 numbers come from the same two-act run; the Rite effects also apply in Act 1 and so are not isolated to Act 2.
